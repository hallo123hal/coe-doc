# CoE Meeting Notes – Wrap up Day 3 notice

Source: https://biplus.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SamsungCoETech/pages/2486829159 (author Tùng Nguyễn Sơn, 2026-10-27)

A short notice of how everyone should work through the remaining handover sessions:
- After each session, practise what was covered, especially Backoffice (BO) tasks, to reinforce learning.
- Review the shared KB articles and confirm you can access, view and download all relevant attachments.
- Create a Confluence page to consolidate your notes, with a separate child page per handover topic (one page per topic with your captured notes).
- Before each topic, prepare questions on the areas to focus on; during or after the session raise follow-up questions.
- By the next day, document the key points taught in detail so the team has a clear reference for future handovers.
