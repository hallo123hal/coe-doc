# CoE Meeting Notes – Office and Security

Source: https://biplus.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SamsungCoETech/pages/2486763651 (author Tùng Nguyễn Sơn, 2026-10-27). The original is in Vietnamese; this is an English summary of observations of the Samsung SDS office during the in-person handover.

## Day 1 (2026-10-27)
- The office floor plan is not large; desks are set up with a spacing similar to the former marketing-team corner (in front of the Innovated room), which is where the current Tech team sits.
- Each person has one extra monitor.
- Entry requires card check-in/check-out (face recognition might work, as currently used).
- All rooms have cameras – at least one per meeting room, covering all angles.
- VPN is issued for remote work to all members and reset every 6 months.
- Chong is the person who monitors SLA, coordinates tickets and checks that current ticket handling is correct.
- **No separate clean room able to hold 40 people was seen (or not found yet).**
- 5 meeting rooms, roughly 5–12 seats each, spread around the office.
- A separate pantry; the network is fully wired LAN, no Wi-Fi.

## Day 2
- The office network seems restricted: Chong said they cannot access Google Meet.
- Meeting rooms have a glass door (frosted) that still allows a look inside to check occupancy; the other sides are solid walls.
- There is no shower; only the shared floor toilets (the SDS office is one unit on a shared floor, not the full floor).

Note: this contrasts with the Introduction page, where RHQ plans a clean room at the VN office (70-person capacity, 50 seats set up) for the next flagship.
