## Main content

### Part A (recording 15:33) – overview and workflow
**What flagship is (01:00–02:00).** Two MX flagships a year: the **S series** (this year S26, S26 Plus, S26 Ultra) and the **Z series** (Fold and Flip, usually July). Accessories too: **Watch normally goes with Z, Buds with S**.

**A strict process (02:00–05:00).**
- First sign a **pledge** to access flagship info. A setup guide exists; subs raise requirements in the Jira project **FLC** (Flagship Launching Communication), a **restricted project** (VN team gets access later).
- Partner **WiseWire (WW)** is the **QA for B2C**; CoE is the **setup** side. CoE must finish **all fundamental setup within one week** (Mon–Fri); QA checks in a **restricted environment**. Then **UAT**: the sub sits in a **clean room**, tests **each SKU** and promotion, all discounts and text on **BC/PD**, buy page, cart to checkout. Before UAT, CoE must self-verify, keep the files used and document everything on the ticket.
- Example ticket **[QHB8][SAVINA] Flagship Setup Verification (FLC-20303)**: WiseWire asks for Product SKU list, Restricted product list, Reference product list, Excluded product list (optional), plus links (Flagship Overall Process, Flagship Set-up Guide, EPP Test Guide, Promotion Review and Sign-Off tracker, BC page URLs).
- WiseWire raises **one consolidated ticket** and checks **A to Z**; all communication goes in the ticket; sub-related questions need the **sub's confirmation**. A **daily call with WiseWire** reports progress. Full info from the subs is needed before setup.

**Subs data sharing status (05:00–06:00).** Page **QBH8 Backend: Subs data sharing Status**: one row per country (NZ, PH, MY, SG, AU, ID, VN, TH), cells RECEIVED / OVERDUE / N/A / OPEN / PARTIAL RECEIVED / NOT RECEIVED with FLC ticket numbers and due dates. Subs provide: **SKU list**, **SC+ list**, **trade-in data** (N-1 model update), **Stock = max pre-order quantity** per model, **RRP**, **pre-order date** (usually from **RHQ**), **SC+ subscription** SKU, **special price**, **merchandising text**.

**Promotions (06:00–11:24).** Mandatory from HQ: **MBO** (multi-buy offer, a PWP-type: buy the main device to get discounted accessories), **Live commerce**, **Hand Raiser (HR)** (teaser before pre-order; customers register on samsung.com and get a **voucher (HR code)**; set up **before** pre-order; value fixed or per model, e.g. S26 Ultra 100, S26 50, S26 Plus 70–80, stackable), always **HR voucher** and **PWP**, plus **FBT** or **MBO** per HQ. Optional: local promotions (e.g. **FOC** case/protector/power bank with a Fold), **Rewards Boost** (N/A if none), **Overtrade**, **RNPL**. Missing info must be **reported to RHQ** or the whole setup slips; CoE logs missing info daily. **Setup starts only after RHQ confirms** ("nothing to be done before they confirm").

**Setup order and HQ guidelines (11:24–13:52).** SKU → SC+ → trade-in mapping → product reference for add-on accessories. **Add-on page is mandatory**; follow the **HQ guide 100%**, new ideas need approval; HQ keeps updating the guidelines. Info is needed for **e-store B2C, EPP and SMB**; say "none"/"No" explicitly. Report on the daily call (RHQ L2, WiseWire, CoE) even when a sub says "no" to a mandatory item.

**COE Product Setup tracker (13:52–22:20).** Page **QBH8 Backend: COE Product Setup**, statuses NOT STARTED / IN PROGRESS / COMPLETED / BLOCKED / N/A; B2C columns: Restricted SKUs Upload, SKUs Upload, Approval Status Update, Sales Status Update, Set IMEI/Serial No, Price (RRP), Special Price (Storage upgrade), Super Sync, Assign Delivery Modes, Sync SKUs to Child Staged Catalog, Upload Product Reference (ACCESSORIES / FBT/MBO / TRADE-IN / SC+ / T-ADDON)…
- Step 1 is **restricted SKU upload**; at upload **approval is check/unapprove** and **sales status Archive** – change nothing.
- **IMEI** for 5G/LTE devices, **serial number** for Wi-Fi only, set in **required additional info of the base code**. SAVINA also sets serial numbers on **accessories** (barcode scan at checkout).
- **RRP via Super Sync**; failures or price mismatch go into the sync-fail report (usually the sub's list and HQ's list differ). Sub-only SKUs (not in the original list) go live **only after all promotions**, e.g. ~3 hours later.
- **Storage upgrade / memory upgrade / LSB** = buy 512GB at the 256GB price, set up as **special price**.
- **Super Sync** must pass (AEM, stock, warehouse). Every SKU needs **delivery modes** and a **sync to child catalogs**: all catalogs (not 3PD, but never forget SMB) to **child Staged and Online**.
- Product reference for accessories (add-on page); **SC+** and **T-ADDON** references (T-add-on = small cart add-on); **AEM Additional Info Type (eVoucher)** (mostly **NZ and AU**); pre-order setup (max quantity from the sub, date from RHQ); payment restriction (usually none); leasing config (RHQ); promotion (usually the longest); merchandising text/flags (few sites); finally **sync child to Online** and **SC+ perks**. **SMB** like B2C; this year **3PD** (approval/sales status, delivery mode, stock allocation) too. **Inventory**: report "no update" if unknown.
- Failed SKUs go to **QBH8 – SKU Super Sync Failure** (AEM Syncing Status table with Sub, SKU list, RHQ/Oliver remarks); e.g. SKUs "to be registered post-unpacked 3hrs+++ as they weren't submitted by sub in original SKU gathering".

**Restricted product setup (22:20–28:40).**
- Products upload as **restricted** (visible only on **P6**). Every SKU to be sold must be in the **restricted product list before upload**.
- Risk: accessories may be **shared with a previous model** (e.g. Watch bands). Restricting a SKU **already on production makes it disappear** (or become unbuyable in a promotion) → search every SKU in backoffice and tell the sub if it already exists.
- 4 steps: (1) prepare data, (2) upload to the FLC ticket for **WW to verify**, (3) upload into BO after FLC approval, (4) WW checks BO. Sample comments: "Kindly assist to verify the Variant SKU and Base SKU in the list RestrictedProduct.xlsx before I upload into hybris", "compare with HQ/RHQ SKU and base code list…". **Shared base code**: restricting it hides the other SKUs with that base code → ask the sub to **change the base code**, have WW re-check, then upload. Advice: "**slow down**", upload only on WW's green light. After upload report e.g. "Total SKU: 182, Base SKU: 48, Variant SKU: 134".
- Upload: store ID first, base code and variant in one column; **Samsung Backoffice Extension → Flagship Progress → Upload Restricted Products** (download the sample Excel). Verify: **Administration Cockpit → Types → RestrictedProduct → Search by Type**. **Do not mix up country codes**; report problems immediately.

**Tracking on FLC tickets (28:40–41:30).**
- Chong logs every sub ticket in a **SKU Setup Update** table pinned on the WiseWire ticket (Supersync, Required additional info, External Service Type, Delivery mode, Max Pre-order Quantity, Tax Class e.g. vn-vat-tax-class-8%, Pre-Order Setup, Benefit, Product Reference TRADEIN / T-ADDON / SC+, Add-On Page, Promotion). Use **labels** to filter your tickets; keep all files used in the ticket. SAVINA: from now the SAVINA PIC says which delivery mode goes to which catalog. **Max pre-order quantity**: a sub wrote 50 for everything – ask again. Tax class: the sub says only "8%"; match the existing class.
- **Benefit** tickets change constantly in UAT ("revise, revise, revise"); QA may check ticket 1 while ticket 2 has already been revised. Chong logs tickets **in order at the end of each day** (first ticket, cart ticket, revisions). Read old tickets once you have access.
- **Product reference applies to all sites of the catalog unless excluded** (column **excludedSites**); done for **EA (Vietnam), e-store and EPP**; e.g. **Remove T-Brand** (FLC-21847). File columns: sourceCode, referenceType (**AVAILABLE_SERVICE**), active (TRUE), targetCode (e.g. **T-ADDON**), excludedSites, startDate, endDate, priority, preSelected, supportedChannels, externalServiceType…. Excluding a site means no T-add-on there. SC+ example (SAVINA): valid 2026-07-22 19:00 to 2026-08-31 23:59, **Exclude site** VN_EA_SHOWCASE, VN_EA_SHOWCASE_LOYALTY, VN_EA_SWL, VN_HIFPT; know which catalog each site uses; if unsure, check the previous flagship and confirm with the sub. Add-on pages have an exclusion list too (SMB is not in the EPP catalog). **Every flagship model has its own add-on page** (S26, S26 Plus, S26 Ultra differ).
- QA screenshots in the clean room must **mask product images/names**, but show **price**. **WiseWire only checks that information is filled**; correctness is CoE's responsibility. WW example result: "Super sync dotcom: Fail for accessories SKUs starting with GP-"; others PASS. Chong: GP- SKUs are not in the accessories gathering file so may not be on AEM; reported to RHQ; resync, then raise a ticket if still failing; for price sync failures ask the sub whether NERP has the price.
- **Sub tickets** (examples): **Add-on Category Filter** and **AEM category** for accessories (identifiers AEM_QBH8B8_RECOMMENDED, _WATCHES, _CASE, _BUDS, _CHARGERS; files vnEstoreProductCatalog.xlsx, vnEaRetailProductCatalog.xlsx; "Marketing Price will be uploaded via **Auto MBO**"); ticket FLC-20380. SAVINA promotion **text is mostly Vietnamese** and changes later. QA example: Eco list 776 SKUs vs 769 in category "RNPL_REDEMPTION-FLC20361-PWP" → 7 missing because "Not found in hybris". **MBO/PWP** rules logged in the ticket; **QA uploads its own QA file** (e.g. "QA Review (FLC-20362) V2.pptx"); comment "Rules Update" (…BOGO_FLC20362-HR-V1AC – COMPLETED, …DISCOUNT…, …PWP…, "Pending Publish and Testing"). **FLC-20362 "Voucher Handraiser Full-form on D2C"**: 22 Jul 20:00 – 17 Aug 23:59 VN time; sites vn, ea, vn_ea_showcase, vn_ea_swl; HR code **V1AC-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx** (made in a VNME ticket), **400,000 codes** for D2C and EPP; GCDM file name **<countrycode>-<voucherquantity>-<prefix>-vouchercodes-<timestamp>_00000000_00000000.csv**.
- **Benefit text on PD shows only 2 lines** (then "…"): put attractive words in the first **10–15 words**. Be careful with images/wording about a **new product**.
- A promotion containing SKUs in the **restricted table** can be **published without Knox approval** (testing only); special price during UAT also needs **no approval** (send the new file). **Start dates must be early**: subs give the pre-order time, which cannot be verified in the clean room; Chong sets special price/discount row/promotion start **around SKU setup time**. Every promotion **needs a start and an end date** (even 5–10 years out) – reports query missing dates.

### Part B (recording 16:52) – detailed setup, approval/freeze, AEM vs Hybris, data handling
**Basic SKU setup (00:00–05:00).**
- Product registration data: **modelCode** = variant SKU (e.g. SM-F956BAKDPHL), **modelName** = base code (SM-F956B), **category** (default 1234, e.g. **MO0703** Galaxy Z); myProductCatalog adds unit (pieces), preOrderDeliveryDate, preOrderTransferToWarehouseDate, isTieredPriceAtBaseProduct. All SKUs must be in the restricted table first.
- **Super Sync (AEM and NERP)**: Product Cockpit → all variant SKUs → **Products Sync Selector** → **DOTCOM, PRICE, STOCK, IMAGE** (image usually not needed) → check the result; retry 1–2 times, then report. Sync to **all child catalogs, Staged and Online**.
- **Base code** needs an **Identifier** (Administration Cockpit → base SKU → Administration); without it the product does not show on the front end. **Required Additional Info**: LTE/5G (SIM tray) → **IMEI**; Wi-Fi tablet/watch or Bluetooth watch → **SERIALNO**.
- **Delivery mode per variant SKU** (PH: ph-delivery-standard, ph-o2o-pickup): assign at **master**, sync to all catalogs, download the **SKU report** to verify.
- **External Service Type**: MX devices **BENEFIT, ACCESSORIES, TRADE_IN, INSURANCE** (no INSURANCE for watches without SC+); other accessories **BENEFIT, ACCESSORIES**. Without BENEFIT the Offers section on BC is empty; without ACCESSORIES the add-on page (Multi Buy Offer) is missing; without TRADE_IN no trade-in; without INSURANCE no Samsung Care+.
- **STOCK (max pre-order)**: stock bulk upload with productCode, warehouse (e.g. WC3L-S513), maxPreOrder; PH also needs a pin map postcode, delivery postcode and product postcode rule map.
- **Checklists**: variant SKU (base code, product type, external service type, delivery mode per catalog, identifier, own price rows (RRP), tax class, approval status, sales status); base SKU (identifier, super categories, approval, sales status, required additional info).

**SC+, Trade-in, Overtrade (05:00–10:14).**
- **SC+ SKU** must follow the latest **standardized SC+ SKU format** (KB STANDARDIZED SC+ SKU Format): formerly **P-GT…**, now regional **SMC-…** (e.g. modelCode P-GT-NXXCS2FC, modelName SMC-MY, category smc; base code differs for bundle / monthly / standalone / standalone monthly). Fundamental setup at master: **Identifier**, **Blocked for stock import = TRUE**, **Blocked for price import = TRUE**.
- **SC+ mapping**: product reference at the **child catalog**: sourceCode = main SKU, **AVAILABLE_SERVICE**, active TRUE, targetCode = SC+ SKU (P-GT-NXXCS2FC 1 year; P-GT-AXXCS2FC 2 years), **excludedSites** if needed; upload to each site's child catalog and sync Staged → Online. Verify: Administration Cockpit → Products → child catalog → "Products referenced by this product"; on BC the SC+ option and in the cart a Samsung Care+ line. Test trade-in, SC+ and T-add-on in the clean room.
- **Trade-in mapping**: every SKU needs **AVAILABLE_SERVICE** to the **T base code** (MY uses T_SMARTPHONE_APPLE, T_SMARTPHONE_SAMSUNG, T_WATCH_…, T_TABLET_… – 29 rows), then sync. Verify in the child catalog; on BC choose a **brand**, then "select model" = **T variant code**. T codes already exist; new models come by sub ticket via the sync job. Check the **Samsung Trade-in** popup on BC and cart.
- **Overtrade (trade-in boost)**: via **master catalog**, upload/download **TRADE_IN_DEVICE_PRICE**: **OfferID** (promotion naming rule, e.g. MY_ESTORE_FLIP6_OVERTRADE_240111_241231_500OFF), **VariantSKU**, **SamsungDiscount** (500), **OpenPromotion TRUE**, **StartDate/EndDate** (sub's local time), **CMSSite** (no site = no overtrade), **serviceBaseProducts**. Full upload sample: KB NZ Enhanced Overtrade. Verify: **User Role Chooser → productmanagerrole** → "overtrade" → **Overtrade Discounts Bonus** → search by code. Key fields: discount amount, start/end, site, service base products, main products, **ExternalServiceDiscountConfig.openDiscount = TRUE**; some service products can be excluded.

**AEM Additional Info Type and obsolete configs (10:14–13:22).** Administration Cockpit → Type **AEMAdditionalInfoTypeData**. Formerly used for display control (e.g. price slash); now **not used, except e-voucher**. **disableCTA** (disable the CTA for the lowest storage) – "NOT USED ANYMORE. DONE ON AEM". **PDP_NOT_AVAILABLE** (key salesStatus) and instant delivery by colour: no longer used (MY still uses it for an RDD issue – check with RHQ). **Delivery message** (pre-order delivery date) is now provided by **GCDM** onto the storefront/BC. Always distinguish AEM work from Hybris work.

**Approval, UAT, freeze (13:22–27:42).**
- Before RHQ's green light B2C and EPP stay **check/unapprove** with sales status **Archive**; change to **APPROVED / PURCHASABLE** only on **RHQ's instruction** (needed before QA can start).
- Q&A: hundreds of SKUs → test each one (read each market carefully). The hardest part is the volume of changes, including prices. After UAT confirmation **raise approval for everything**: record every promotion, bulk approvals; prices need approval again. Always hold the **latest price file** (subs change prices daily); using an old file is wrong.
- At the end of UAT and approval submission Hybris enters **system freeze**: nothing can change without approval. After one day of **soft freeze** comes **hard freeze**; promotions get approved; then re-check everything in the **clean room for 2 days** (QA and merchandising **sanity check** with the latest guide).
- Record changes **at the end of each day** in time order. **QA must test against the sub's request file**, not what merchandising says; both must read the brief independently and compare. Chong holds a **session with the sub PIC before setup** to understand the whole scheme (SG example: RNPL, hand raiser, BOGO/FOC for S26 Ultra, payment promotion).
- **Price reconciliation before submitting approval**: retrieve data from the system and compare with the price file submitted (**40–60 thousand price rows per site**); tech can export by script, but the format differs, so a matching tool is needed. Only submit when both match; otherwise resubmit. Pre-order prices may differ by period (first 10 days one price, next 10 another).
- Promotions: re-check **start/end**; export from Hybris; ensure they trigger in freeze. Not every promotion runs the whole pre-order (~3 weeks); schemes can change weekly (SAVINA often has an extra 72-hour promo). Tech can **pull a list of everything set up by keyword** (promotion name / product benefit) for checking.
- Changes during freeze are possible **only with HQ PIC approval** (e.g. a sub gave wrong information). **CMS shop app and EPP CMS are locked** → publish before freeze; after freeze the EPP icon turns **red**, home and offer pages are locked; at grey you may set up but **not publish** (a wrong publish could put flagship content online).
- Common errors: wrong promotion start/end; promotion not QA'd then found not running. Unused benefits: archive and note on the ticket. Promotions that are no longer needed (names cannot change): **set the end date before the flagship** so an accidental publish does not run. Subs asking for an "immediate change": require a **comment on the FLC ticket** – **no comment, no brief, no action**; if urgent, note it on the ticket and tag the requester.

**Training, naming, and AEM vs Hybris (31:00–47:10).**
- Chong runs an **A–Z training before every flagship**. Flow: setup → **self-QA** → QA run → sub **UAT** → EPP work or changes → EPP go live → changes; publish EPP while still restricted on **P6**. At the end of UAT resubmit all promotions: **tech lists all promotion rules set up in the period by keyword** and everyone confirms (BOGO need not, per the speech). Note the **FLC ticket number** and e.g. "HR voucher" + coupon code in the rule. Flagship is **easier than normal tickets** because setup is straightforward; Chong has ~5 flagships (VN 1, PH 2, MY 1, TH 1). Example ticket **[SAVINA] RNPL PDP TEXT (FLC-20668)**.
- **B2C buying page**: most **text comes from AEM** (blue text, option names); CoE cannot change B2C → the sub raises a ticket to **AEM** (an AEM team supports UAT). **EPP**: CoE edits in **EPP CMS**. Wrong colour name → ask **NERP** (data may be mistyped then synced). Trade-in section appears only with the external service type; the frame is AEM-controlled, but check the SKU first; the commonest issue in the first two days is **trade-in not working**. Selling options (SKUs) come from **Hybris**; instalments from **perks / payment provider parameter**; **Offers/benefit** fully from Hybris (whole section missing → check BENEFIT external service type, then the benefit setup and SKU assignment); **special price** from Hybris. **CTA**: "pre-order" during pre-order, "continue" with an add-on page; CTA text changes go to AEM.
- **EPP buying page in AEM PIM** (login with SingleID → Adobe Experience Manager → **PIM → EPP (ver.2) → Flagship BC**): list by Site, Store ID, Group Name (e.g. Galaxy S26 Ultra), UX Design 2.0, E-store Hybris, Deploy Status, Device/Service Option. All multi-store EPP BCs are configured here (search by store ID, e.g. my_src_id). **Option Mapping** tab: Apply Info (Site, Group Name, Category Mobile > Smartphones > Galaxy Z, URL /my/multistore/my_src_id/…, EPP Use, E-store Hybris, UX Design 2.0), Device Option (DEVICE, STORAGE, EXCLUSIVE COLOR), **View B2C** (read only). **Service Option**: TRADE-IN, SAMSUNG CARE+, SAMSUNG ADVANTAGE (6 items), ADD-ON; trade-in text and the maximum discount amount are edited here; untick **Display**, save and publish to hide a whole section. Mandatory **compare model** and **cross-category navigation** (AEM for e-store, CoE for EPP). **Model Mgmt** sets the default model (Pre-selected Type Use: Rep. Model, Priority Rep. Model, Device Rep. Model, No); banners (three drop-down banners for PC); texts like "25% off + Bundle Offers" under Option Mapping → Device; **Storage**: tick **LSB target** to grey out that storage; colours normally unchanged; SC+ text and offer management are editable (Galaxy Club absent).

**Data and work management (47:10–55:00).** On **Unpacked** day support go-live and **check live**. The biggest challenge is managing data flow and change requests. Build your own checklist. Chong keeps **one folder per ticket number** (FLC-20361 RNPL, FLC-20362 HR FullForm, FLC-20366 MBO WATCH, FLC-20369 PAYMENT PROMO, FLC-20380 ADDON PAGE, FLC-20382 SPECIAL PRICE…). A **central tracking file** for QA (sheets NOTES, Preorder Period, **Supersync** SUCCESS/FAILURE/SKIPPED, Base Code, PRICE, **PROMO**: ticket, scheme, site, main/child SKU, voucher/coupon type, test voucher code, start/end local, priority, rule name, purpose; cancelled promotions are **struck through, not deleted**). Chong will share the files via one ticket. Previous flagship tickets carry labels **COE_FLAGSHIP**, **COE_QBH8** etc. in FLC – spend **one day** downloading all of it. Stay calm, update everything daily, wait for **RHQ** instructions, and stay inside the guideline.

## Q&A (55:00 – end of recording 2)
- **SLA?** Normal tickets have an SLA; **flagship (FLC) tickets have no SLA**. Move flagship work raised in normal projects to FLC. **Testing tickets** before flagship (PH, MY) are allowed in the normal project (test on staging and report); ingest-file / voucher-code ingest tickets with flagship keywords also appear beforehand. Near flagship, **sensitive info** (SKU, base code, names, assets) must **not** be in normal tickets, only FLC; ask **RHQ** for support (e.g. images).
- **Which catalogs?** Your own country's catalogs; **not 3PD, but SMB yes**.
- **EPP has several schemes** (TH: one set for **internal**, one for **external EPP**, one for **aggregator** – three setups). Set up one EPP and **clone** it to sites in the same group when setups are **identical** (Internal = A, B, C; External = D, E, F; Edu = G, H, I); sites in a group can differ (e.g. A has Samsung Rewards, B not) and then must be done one by one, so the volume can be huge. Chong will share an example and add missing details next week.

## Difficult points and open issues
- Only **one week** for fundamental setup before UAT, and only once **RHQ permits**; subs' info is late or wrong (max pre-order 50 for all).
- **Restricted product**: shared SKUs/base codes (watch bands) already on production vanish when restricted; check every SKU and wait for WiseWire's confirmation.
- **Change management in UAT**: benefits, text and prices keep changing; the old file is easily used; QA and merchandising diverge. Log changes by ticket order daily.
- **Price reconciliation** (40–60k rows per site) lacks a tool; needs tech support.
- **Freeze**: after approval Hybris and EPP CMS are locked; changes need HQ approval; a wrong publish could expose flagship content.
- **AEM vs Hybris** split: many B2C texts only AEM can change; EPP is in PIM by CoE; some AEM Additional Info configs are obsolete (MY still uses one for RDD – ask RHQ).
- Unclear speech: GSM "except Vietnam", BOGO "not needed" when resubmitting rules; slides 3 and 5 of Restricted Product were not detailed.
