### MER-PRC-01 - Price upload (Tier 1 #8, Must, Sprint 2, owner Tùng)

https://biplus.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SamsungCoETech/pages/2483631159

## What the service does
The requester gets new or changed **regular, RRP and always-on (AON) prices** live at an exact date/time in the market time zone, approved in Knox where required, and checked by a second person on PDP, cart and checkout. A missing zero, wrong time zone or a price live before approval sells at a loss within minutes on every site that shares it.

### MER-PRM-01 - Product discount promotion (fixed price, percentage) (Tier 1 #10, Must, Sprint 3, owner Thành)

https://biplus.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SamsungCoETech/pages/2483630781

## What the service does
A **product-level discount rule** (target fixed price, percentage off or fixed amount off on named SKUs or categories) built in the Hybris promotion engine, live at the exact start date-time in market time and ended on time. A wrong value, missing exclusion or unplanned stack sells devices below the approved price on many sites at once.

Full summary: [Day3_Thu-29-Oct/1100_RoomA_t46_all-promotions-priority-scheme-promoti.md](Day3_Thu-29-Oct/1100_RoomA_t46_all-promotions-priority-scheme-promoti.md)

### MER-PRC-02 - Special price setup (Tier 1 #11, Must, Sprint 3, owner Tùng)

https://biplus.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SamsungCoETech/pages/2483598300

## What the service does
The requester gets a **time-bound special selling price**, shown against the RRP, for a campaign window on the requested sites. Approved in Knox and checked by a second person at the **start and at the end** of the window. Overlap, stacking with a promotion, wrong start/end or a price that stays after the window are direct margin loss.

Full summary: [Day1_Tue-27-Oct/1050_RoomA_t15_knox-approval.md](Day1_Tue-27-Oct/1050_RoomA_t15_knox-approval.md), [Day3_Thu-29-Oct/1130_RoomA_t44_discount-row.md](Day3_Thu-29-Oct/1130_RoomA_t44_discount-row.md)
