## What the service does
The requester gets a **free gift added to the cart automatically at zero price**, only when a qualifying main product is bought, on the requested sites and for an exact period. The gift is a **free-gift promotion rule**. A "must purchase with" reference (MUST_PURCHASE_WITH) is used *only* for a mandatory companion (e.g. indoor + outdoor air-conditioner units). A wrong rule gives stock away, misses a promised gift, or gives it with the wrong product, quantity or site.

## MER-STK-01 - Super Sync inventory from NERP

## What the service does
Hybris is brought back in line with the source systems for the listed SKUs - **NERP warehouse stock, NERP RRP, or AEM product content** - pushed to the master and child catalogs with **Super Sync**. Without it SKUs stay out of stock while stock sits in the warehouse, keep selling virtual stock that no longer exists, or show an old price/name.

## Recognise the ticket
Titles: *Activate & Sync Warehouse Stock, Remove/Release Dummy Setup & Sync, Supersync SKU*. V2 tickets follow the due date of a MER-STK-02 / STK-03 ticket. **Not this service:** setting virtual stock -> MER-STK-02 - pre-order attributes -> MER-STK-03 - activate/archive -> MER-PRD-01 - price list -> MER-PRC-01 - sync job failure, warehouse mapping, reserved stock -> Tech (TEC-TSP-06, TEC-SIT-03, TEC-MON-02) - a *new* SKU registration is MER-PRD-01 V1 (Super Sync is only a step there).

Variants: **V1 activate and sync warehouse stock** (~36%, MY by division) - V2 release virtual stock and hand back to NERP stock - V3 Super Sync RRP - V4 Super Sync AEM content (DOTCOM, IMAGE) - V5 re-sync after failure / late source.

## Workflow (key points)
- **Source ready first**: stock in the NERP storage location that feeds the eStore warehouse, RRP effective in NERP, content published in AEM - else one clarification (ON HOLD SUBS).
- Super Sync = Product Cockpit > Products > master catalog > select SKUs > toolbar **Products Sync Selector** > data type DOTCOM / PRICE / STOCK / IMAGE (AU also SMARTTHING), then Synchronize master -> child Staged -> Online.
- V1: before export (block flags Q1, Stock Level Q2) -> activate (PRD-01) -> **unblock stock import** in every catalog (`blockStockUpdatesFromExternalSystems` = False; MX also GSCM flag) -> Super Sync STOCK -> compare `available` with the NERP figure -> sync -> PDP.
- V2 handback: remove the virtual quantity (available = 0 on `VirtualWarehouse-<cc>`), remove the Stock Import Override entry, unblock, Super Sync STOCK; if reserved stays and the SKU shows no stock, **do not clear it** - COEC for TEC-MON-02. Pre-order SKUs: remove pre-order under STK-03 first; **never run Super Sync STOCK on a pre-order SKU**.
- V3 RRP: check price block flag (`blockPriceUpdatesFromExternalSystems` stopped 20 ID SKUs in B7Q7), PS-03/PS-04 sanity, flag dependent special prices/promotions; QA sample (~10%, min 3 per catalog type). Bulk lists >100 rows need a diff file (PS-13).
- Stock import job runs about every 30 min (TH 3x/hour). Same SKUs fail twice -> COEC for TEC-TSP-06 with both result lists.

## Known errors
Out of stock after sync (reserved left on the virtual warehouse) - sync brings no stock (import still blocked in one catalog / override entry) - NERP has stock, Hybris 0 (storage location not mapped) - RRP unchanged (NERP not effective / price block flag) - 1-cent difference in child catalogs (rounding) - AEM sync failed (not published / not in SKU Gathering) - SKU on the wrong EPP site after activation.

## QA checklist
Source confirmed ready; scope and sites complete; before export; import unblocked in all catalogs; no virtual quantity / override left (V2); available matches NERP; reserved reviewed; RRP equals list (V3); failed SKUs listed with reason; storefront sample; diff file for bulk >100 rows.
